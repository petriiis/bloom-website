Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set my quit date?
When you first open Bloom, you'll be guided through onboarding where you enter your quit date. You can update it later in Settings → Profile if you need to change it.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
When you download Bloom, you automatically get 7 days of full Premium access — no credit card required. After the trial, you can subscribe to keep full access or continue with the free tier (which includes basic tracking).
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open your iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → find Bloom → Cancel. You'll keep premium access until the end of your billing period. You can also manage subscriptions through the App Store.
I bought premium but lost access after reinstalling. How do I restore?
Open Bloom → Settings → Restore Purchase. Your purchase is linked to your Apple ID and will be restored automatically. If you used a gift code, sign in with the same account you used when redeeming.
What is a Gift Code and how do I redeem one?
A Bloom Gift Code gives lifetime premium access as a gift. To redeem: open the app → Settings → scroll to "Redeem Gift Code" → enter your code. Codes look like BLOOM-22-XXXX-XXXX.
How does the Sakura Garden work?
Your virtual cherry blossom tree grows automatically as you stay smoke-free. It starts as a seed and blooms through 10 stages over weeks and months. The Garden tab shows your current stage and what's coming next.
What is the AI Quit Coach?
The Bloom Coach is an AI assistant powered by Google's Gemini model that specializes in helping people quit smoking. Ask it anything — cravings, withdrawal, motivation, or what's happening in your body. It's available 24/7 in the Learn tab.
How does the Craving SOS work?
The SOS tab gives you instant tools when a craving hits: Emergency Breathing (4-7-8 technique), a Craving Timer, Motivation Boost, your personal Reasons to Quit, and a Distraction Game. Most cravings pass within 3-5 minutes.
How are my health milestones calculated?
Bloom tracks 300+ real health milestones based on scientific research into smoking cessation. Timings are adjusted based on how many cigarettes you smoked per day. The milestones show the actual physical recovery happening in your body.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase and is never sold to third parties. You can request deletion of all your data at any time by emailing support@bloomsmokefree.com. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
The app is not calculating my savings correctly. What should I check?
Go to Settings → Profile and verify your cigarettes per day and cost per pack are correct. The savings calculation is based on these numbers × days smoke-free. Make sure you've entered the full pack cost (not per cigarette).
I had a slip and smoked. What should I do?
A slip doesn't mean failure — it's part of the journey for many people. You can reset your quit date in Settings → Profile. Many successful quitters had setbacks before quitting for good. The important thing is to keep going. 💙
Can I use Bloom without an internet connection?
Yes, most features work offline — progress tracking, SOS tools, and your diary. The AI Quit Coach requires an internet connection. Your data will sync when you reconnect.
How do I share my progress?
On the Today tab, tap the share icon next to "My Progress" to generate a beautiful shareable card with your stats. You can also share your Garden from the Garden tab.
