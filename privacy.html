Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Petros Michael ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Bloom app and the bloomsmokefree.com website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name (for personalization within the app)
  • Quit date and smoking habits (cigarettes per day, cost per pack)
  • Diary entries and mood logs you choose to record
  • Reasons to quit and savings goals you set
  • Messages you send to the AI Quit Coach

Information collected automatically

  • Firebase Authentication: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and a unique identifier. We do not receive your password.
  • Firebase Firestore: Your app data (progress, goals, diary entries) is stored securely in Google Firebase.
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage data such as screens viewed and features used, to help us improve the app.
  • In-App Purchases: Processed entirely by Apple. We receive confirmation of purchase status but never your payment details.

Website analytics

Our website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. No data is shared with third parties for advertising purposes. You can view Plausible's data policy at plausible.io/privacy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize the Bloom app experience
  • To track your smoke-free progress and calculate health milestones
  • To power the AI Quit Coach (messages are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing)
  • To send you notifications and reminders you have opted into
  • To restore your data if you reinstall the app
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns
  • To process premium subscription verification via Apple StoreKit

3. AI Quit Coach

Messages you send to the Bloom AI Quit Coach are processed by Google's Gemini API. Messages may be temporarily processed on Google's servers to generate responses. We do not permanently store your coaching conversations on our servers beyond your device and Firebase account. Please do not share sensitive personal or medical information with the AI Coach.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase, for authentication, data storage, and analytics
  • Google Gemini API, for AI coaching responses
  • Apple, for in-app purchase verification
  • Plausible Analytics, for anonymous website analytics

All third-party services are bound by their own privacy policies and GDPR-compliant data processing agreements.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@bloomsmokefree.com. We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access, Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification, Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure, Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to restriction, Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to portability, Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to object, Object to our processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@bloomsmokefree.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Bloom is not intended for children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If we become aware that a child under 17 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Firebase data is stored in Google's secure infrastructure with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: support@bloomsmokefree.com
  • Website: bloomsmokefree.com/support
